# Predis.ai — image workflow source notes

Accessed: 2026-09-15

## User-supplied demo screenshots

The supplied sequence shows these product surfaces:

- onboarding that derives a brand profile from a website and confirms logo, colors, font and audience;
- a creation router with Product Ad Video, UGC, Faceless Video, Image, Carousel and Product Photo Shoot;
- four image entry routes: idea, store/CSV, product URL and product-image upload;
- product confirmation with description, price, offer, instructions and a selectable product-asset grid;
- optional reference-ad remixing;
- output ratio selection and brand application;
- a layer-based creative editor with text, shapes, buttons, stock media, uploads, AI images, icons, background, effects and product controls;
- a content library filtered by image/video/carousel;
- publishing, calendar, auto-posting and performance surfaces downstream from creation.

The screenshots are product-observation evidence, not proof of output quality or performance.

## Official sources

1. https://predis.ai/developers/docs/predis-sdk/API%20reference/createPost/
   - Official SDK types distinguish image, video, carousel, product_photo_shoot, UGC and other post types.
   - The create flow can receive custom image assets and brand details such as logo and colors.

2. https://predis.ai/ai-social-media-post-generator/
   - Official product page describes generation of images, videos, carousels, captions and hashtags from one idea.
   - It describes website/past-post brand learning, a built-in editor, download, scheduling and direct publishing.

3. https://predis.ai/ecommerce-ad-maker/
   - Official product page describes starting from a product/store URL or one product photo.
   - It says the system reads images, price and description, applies brand settings, creates static/video ads, supports catalog batches and reference-ad reuse, and lets users swap frame content.

4. https://predis.ai/use-cases/instagram-ad-maker/
   - Official product page describes prompt/URL input, brand application, static/video/carousel outputs, placement sizing, batch variants and editing before download or scheduling.

All product and performance statements remain vendor claims unless independently verified. This audit uses the sources to understand workflow and information architecture, not to validate marketing metrics.
